<h1>🧠 New Memory Patient</h1>

<p>Use this guide for a patient’s initial evaluation in the Memory Disorders Clinic.</p>

<div class="guide-meta">
  <span class="guide-tag">Initial visit</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Memory clinic</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Checklist</span>
</div>


</div>

<details class="guide-dropdown" open>
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Before Dr. Morgan Enters</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Required intake forms and materials</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Complete the MoCA using the materials in the purple folder.
- [ ] Complete the FAQ using the materials in the purple folder.
- [ ] Complete the PHQ-9 using the materials in the purple folder.
- [ ] Complete the GAD-7 using the materials in the purple folder.
- [ ] Bring a pen and clipboard into the room.
- [ ] For memory patients located in Georgia, obtain the GMN consent form by asking Caty or the social worker.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Before Entering the Room</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Review the chart and prepare for intake</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review the reason for referral.
- [ ] Review previous neurology, memory clinic, or primary care notes.
- [ ] Find the most recent brain MRI or CT.
- [ ] Review available laboratory results.
- [ ] Review the current medication list.
- [ ] Identify the family member or caregiver who can provide collateral history.
- [ ] Review prior cognitive testing, if available.
- [ ] Identify any testing or referrals that have already been completed.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">During the Patient and Caregiver History</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Obtain symptom, functional, and safety history</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Determine when the cognitive symptoms began.
- [ ] Ask whether the symptoms began gradually or suddenly.
- [ ] Ask how the symptoms have progressed.
- [ ] Ask for specific examples of short-term memory difficulty.
- [ ] Ask about repeated questions or repeated conversations.
- [ ] Ask about missed appointments or difficulty keeping track of dates.
- [ ] Ask about misplaced belongings.
- [ ] Ask about difficulty remembering recent events.
- [ ] Ask about changes in language or word-finding.
- [ ] Ask about difficulty recognizing people or objects.
- [ ] Ask about changes in navigation or getting lost.
- [ ] Ask about changes in judgment, planning, or problem-solving.
- [ ] Ask about changes in personality or behavior.
- [ ] Ask about mood, anxiety, depression, or irritability.
- [ ] Ask about hallucinations or delusions.
- [ ] Ask about sleep, including dream enactment or excessive daytime sleepiness.
- [ ] Ask about tremor, stiffness, slowness, gait changes, or falls.
- [ ] Ask about urinary or bowel changes when relevant.
- [ ] Obtain collateral history from a family member or caregiver whenever available.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Function and Safety</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Review ADLs, IADLs, driving, and supervision</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review basic activities of daily living.
- [ ] Review instrumental activities of daily living.
- [ ] Ask who manages medications.
- [ ] Ask who manages finances.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient cooks independently.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient shops independently.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient uses a calendar or reminders.
- [ ] Confirm whether the patient is currently driving.
- [ ] Ask about recent accidents, tickets, getting lost, or family concerns about driving.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient lives alone.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient requires supervision.
- [ ] Ask about falls, wandering, firearm access, or other safety concerns when relevant.
- [ ] Ask about caregiver concerns and caregiver burden.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Examination and Cognitive Testing</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Complete and review the requested assessment</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review the MoCA score and areas of difficulty.
- [ ] Review the FAQ score and reported functional changes.
- [ ] Review the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 results.
- [ ] Complete the appropriate neurologic examination.
- [ ] Assess gait when appropriate.
- [ ] Identify the most important positive examination findings.
- [ ] Compare the patient’s reported symptoms with the caregiver’s collateral history.
- [ ] Identify whether the presentation appears primarily cognitive, psychiatric, functional, neurologic, or mixed.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Presenting the Patient</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Prepare a concise presentation for Dr. Morgan</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] State the patient’s age and reason for referral.
- [ ] Summarize the onset and progression of cognitive symptoms.
- [ ] Provide two or three specific examples of the patient’s cognitive difficulty.
- [ ] Summarize the caregiver’s collateral history.
- [ ] Describe changes in ADLs and IADLs.
- [ ] State who manages medications and finances.
- [ ] State whether the patient is driving.
- [ ] Summarize relevant mood, sleep, behavioral, hallucination, gait, or movement symptoms.
- [ ] Report the MoCA, FAQ, PHQ-9, and GAD-7 scores.
- [ ] Summarize relevant imaging, laboratory testing, and prior neuropsychological testing.
- [ ] Report the most important examination findings.
- [ ] Identify the primary clinical question for the visit.
- [ ] Ask Caty, the fellow, or Dr. Morgan when instructions or findings are unclear.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">After the Visit</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Complete follow-up instructions and documentation</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Confirm ordered laboratory testing.
- [ ] Confirm ordered brain imaging.
- [ ] Confirm whether neuropsychological testing was ordered.
- [ ] Confirm any referrals placed during the visit.
- [ ] Confirm medication recommendations or medication changes.
- [ ] Confirm when the patient should return to clinic via Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Complete the first-patient visit sheet with month (ex: 4 months) and cross out/write down what needs to be done based on plan before the next visit.
- [ ] Inform the patient that testing should be completed before the follow-up appointment.
- [ ] Record where the patient should return if the follow-up visit is not with Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Return to patient room and give the patient the clinic information sheet, first visit sheet, tell them when to return, and to head to the front desk to schedule their next visit.

</div>
</details>

<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Important</strong>

Complete all required questionnaires before Dr. Morgan enters the room. Place a patient sticker on the front of all documents that need to be scanned, then place them in the pink folder.
