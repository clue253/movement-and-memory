# New Movement Patient

Use this checklist for a patient being evaluated in the Movement Disorders Clinic for the first time.

## Before Entering the Room

- [ ] Review the referral reason.
- [ ] Review available neurology notes.
- [ ] Review relevant imaging and testing.
- [ ] Review the medication list.
- [ ] Identify the patient's primary concern.

## Patient History

- [ ] Determine when the symptoms began.
- [ ] Ask how the symptoms have progressed.
- [ ] Ask which body area was affected first.
- [ ] Ask about tremor, stiffness, slowness, abnormal movements, or balance problems.
- [ ] Ask about falls and use of assistive devices.
- [ ] Ask about changes in walking.
- [ ] Ask about swallowing or speech changes.
- [ ] Ask about sleep symptoms.
- [ ] Ask about hallucinations or behavioral changes when relevant.
- [ ] Ask about current and previous treatments.
- [ ] Ask whether medications improve the symptoms.
- [ ] Review basic and instrumental activities of daily living.
- [ ] Confirm whether the patient is driving.

## Examination and Presentation

- [ ] Complete the appropriate neurologic and movement examination.
- [ ] Identify the most important positive findings.
- [ ] Prepare a brief presentation for Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Ask Caty, the fellow, or Dr. Morgan about unclear findings.

## After the Visit

- [ ] Confirm the treatment plan.
- [ ] Confirm medication changes.
- [ ] Confirm referrals or testing orders.
- [ ] Confirm when the patient should return.
- [ ] Complete the follow-up section of the visit sheet.
- [ ] Give the patient the clinic information sheet.
