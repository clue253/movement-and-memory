<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>General Clinic Workflow</strong>

The majority of the new patient workflow is shared between the Memory and Movement Disorders clinics. Please review the **<a href="../memory/new-patient/">Memory Disorders – New Patient Guide</a>** before using this page. The sections below describe only workflow and documentation that differ for Movement Disorders patients.

</div>


<details class="guide-dropdown" open>
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Before Dr. Morgan and YOU Enter the Room</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Required intake forms and materials</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review the referral reason.
- [ ] Review available neurology notes.
- [ ] Review relevant imaging and testing.
- [ ] Review the medication list.
- [ ] Identify the patient's primary concern.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">During the Patient and Caregiver History</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Obtain symptom, functional, and safety history</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review the medication list with patient, relevant to memory, movement, and mood. Disregard all other medications.
- [ ] Write with a header and bullet list below. One header should be: Current Medications, Trialed Medications. Please the medications where needed, and include dosages, time taken, or reason for stopping medication.
- [ ] Determine when the symptoms began.
- [ ] Ask how the symptoms have progressed.
- [ ] Ask which body area was affected first.
- [ ] Ask about tremor, stiffness, slowness, abnormal movements, or balance problems.
- [ ] Ask about falls and use of assistive devices.
- [ ] Ask about changes in walking.
- [ ] Ask about swallowing or speech changes.
- [ ] Ask about sleep symptoms.
- [ ] Ask about hallucinations or behavioral changes when relevant.
- [ ] Ask about current and previous treatments.
- [ ] Ask whether medications improve the symptoms.
- [ ] Review basic and instrumental activities of daily living.
- [ ] Confirm whether the patient is driving.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Presenting the Patient</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Prepare a concise presentation for Dr. Morgan</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] State the patient's age, handedness (if relevant), and reason for referral.
- [ ] Summarize the chief complaint and timeline of symptom onset and progression.
- [ ] Describe the primary movement disorder (e.g., tremor, parkinsonism, dystonia, ataxia, chorea, myoclonus, tics).
- [ ] Describe how the symptoms affect activities of daily living, work, gait, balance, or quality of life.
- [ ] Report associated neurologic symptoms (e.g., falls, freezing, dysphagia, hypophonia, autonomic symptoms, cognitive changes, REM sleep behavior disorder) when applicable.
- [ ] Review current and previous movement disorder medications, including benefit, side effects, wearing off, dyskinesias, and medication timing when applicable.
- [ ] Summarize relevant imaging or diagnostic studies, including DaTscan, MRI brain, EMG/NCS, genetic testing, or other pertinent testing when available.
- [ ] Report the most important examination findings.
- [ ] State your leading differential diagnosis or primary clinical concern, if appropriate.
- [ ] Identify the primary clinical question or reason for today's visit.
- [ ] Clarify any uncertain findings or questions with Caty, the fellow, or Dr. Morgan before presenting the patient.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">After the Visit</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Complete follow-up instructions and documentation</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Confirm the treatment plan.
- [ ] Confirm medication changes.
- [ ] Confirm referrals or testing orders.
- [ ] Confirm when the patient should return with Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Write on clinic information sheet when to follow-up on the visit sheet. Also include any tests needed to be completed before next visit.
- [ ] Give the patient any referrals needed with Dr. Morgans signature. If they are digital referrals, they will receive a call to schedule.
- [ ] Return to patient room and give the patient the clinic information sheet, tell them when to return, and to head to the front desk to schedule their next visit.
</div>
</details>



<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Important</strong>

Complete all required questionnaires before Dr. Morgan enters the room. Documents to scan should have a patient sticker placed on them (the front). Please place them in the pink folder.
