<h1>Second Movement Visit</h1>

<p>Use this checklist for a patient's second Movement Disorders Clinic visit.</p>

<div class="guide-meta">
  <span class="guide-tag">Initial visit</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Memory clinic</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Checklist</span>
</div>

</div>

<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Shared Clinic Workflow</strong>

This guide assumes you are familiar with the general second-visit workflow. If you have not already done so, begin with the <a href="../memory/second-visit/">Memory Disorders – Second Visit Guide</a>. The sections below highlight only the workflow, documentation, and clinical considerations that differ for Movement Disorders patients.

</div>


<details class="guide-dropdown" open>
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Before Dr. Morgan and YOU Enter the Room</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Required intake forms and materials</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Review the previous clinic note.
- [ ] Review the working diagnosis.
- [ ] Review tests, imaging, laboratory results, and referrals ordered at the first visit.
- [ ] Determine whether all requested testing was completed.
- [ ] Review medication changes made at the first visit.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">During the Patient and Caregiver History</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Obtain symptom, functional, and safety history</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Ask whether symptoms have improved, worsened, or remained stable.
- [ ] Ask about new symptoms.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient started the recommended medications.
- [ ] Ask whether the medications have helped.
- [ ] Ask about side effects or wearing off of medication.
- [ ] Ask about falls, walking, swallowing, speech, and daily functioning.
- [ ] Confirm whether the patient is driving.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">Presenting the Patient</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Prepare a concise presentation for Dr. Morgan</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] State the patient's diagnosis and the reason for today's follow-up visit.
- [ ] Summarize any significant changes since the last visit, including progression or improvement of symptoms.
- [ ] Review current movement disorder medications, including medication timing, benefit, wearing off, dyskinesias, adverse effects, and any recent medication changes.
- [ ] Review any interval events, including falls, freezing, dysphagia, speech changes, hospitalizations, emergency department visits, or new diagnoses.
- [ ] Report any interval procedures or treatments, including botulinum toxin injections, DBS programming, focused ultrasound (FUS), physical therapy, occupational therapy, or speech therapy, when applicable.
- [ ] Summarize any new imaging, DaTscan, laboratory results, or diagnostic testing completed since the previous visit.
- [ ] Report the most important neurologic and movement examination findings, emphasizing changes from the previous visit.
- [ ] Describe any changes in activities of daily living, mobility, gait, balance, or quality of life.
- [ ] Identify the primary clinical question or management issue for today's visit.
- [ ] Clarify any uncertain findings or questions with Caty, the fellow, or Dr. Morgan before presenting the patient.

</div>
</details>

<details class="guide-dropdown">
<summary>
  <span class="dropdown-title">After the Visit</span>
  <span class="dropdown-hint">Complete follow-up instructions and documentation</span>
</summary>

<div class="guide-dropdown-content" markdown="1">

- [ ] Confirm Dr. Morgan's assessment and treatment plan.
- [ ] Confirm any medication changes, including dosing instructions and titration schedules.
- [ ] Confirm any new referrals, imaging, laboratory tests, or other diagnostic studies ordered today.
- [ ] Confirm whether additional therapies (e.g., physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, botulinum toxin injections, DBS evaluation, or focused ultrasound evaluation) were recommended, when applicable.
- [ ] Confirm when the patient should return to see Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Complete the clinic information sheet, including the follow-up timeframe and any testing or referrals that should be completed before the next visit.
- [ ] Provide the patient with any signed paper referrals. Inform the patient that electronic referrals will contact them directly to schedule.
- [ ] Return to patient room and give the patient the clinic information sheet, tell them when to return (with it writen on the clinic infromation sheet), and to head to the front desk to schedule their next visit.
</div>
</details>


<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Important</strong>

Complete all required questionnaires before Dr. Morgan enters the room. Documents to scan should have a patient sticker placed on them (the front). Please place them in the pink folder.
