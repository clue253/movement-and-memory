# Second Movement Visit

Use this checklist for a patient's second Movement Disorders Clinic visit.

<div class="guide-meta">
  <span class="guide-tag">Initial visit</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Memory clinic</span>
  <span class="guide-tag">Checklist</span>
</div>

</div>

<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Shared Clinic Workflow</strong>

This guide assumes you are familiar with the general second-visit workflow. If you have not already done so, begin with the <a href="../memory/second-visit/">Memory Disorders – Second Visit Guide</a>. The sections below highlight only the workflow, documentation, and clinical considerations that differ for Movement Disorders patients.

</div>

</div>

<div class="guide-section">

## Before Dr. Morgan Enters

- [ ] Review the previous clinic note.
- [ ] Review the working diagnosis.
- [ ] Review tests, imaging, laboratory results, and referrals ordered at the first visit.
- [ ] Determine whether all requested testing was completed.
- [ ] Review medication changes made at the first visit.

</div>

<div class="guide-section">

## During the History

- [ ] Ask whether symptoms have improved, worsened, or remained stable.
- [ ] Ask about new symptoms.
- [ ] Ask whether the patient started the recommended medications.
- [ ] Ask whether the medications have helped.
- [ ] Ask about side effects or wearing off of medication.
- [ ] Ask about falls, walking, swallowing, speech, and daily functioning.
- [ ] Confirm whether the patient is driving.

</div>

<div class="guide-section">

## Presenting the Patient

- [ ] Complete the appropriate follow-up examination.
- [ ] Compare the examination with the previous visit.
- [ ] Prepare a brief presentation for Dr. Morgan, including medication.
- [ ] Be prepared to discuss the likely diagnosis and response to treatment.

</div>

<div class="guide-section">

## After the Visit

- [ ] Confirm the treatment plan.
- [ ] Confirm medication changes.
- [ ] Confirm referrals or testing orders.
- [ ] Confirm when the patient should return with Dr. Morgan.
- [ ] Write on clinic information sheet when to follow-up on the visit sheet.
- [ ] Return to patient room and give the patient the clinic information sheet, tell them when to return, and to head to the front desk to schedule their next visit.

</div>

<div class="guide-callout">

<strong>Important</strong>

Complete all required questionnaires before Dr. Morgan enters the room. Documents to scan should have a patient sticker placed on them (the front). Please place them in the pink folder.

</div>
